WHAT WE DO

Integrated site and engineering services.

One partner across design, construction, maintenance, industrial support and logistics — with a strong focus on safe execution and client requirements.

01

Construction & Civil Works

Building works, plants, metallic construction, fuel stations and housing, including painting, brickwork, woodwork, windows, doors, plumbing, electrical work and welding.

02

HDPE & Geomembrane Systems

Supply and installation of HDPE geomembranes, engineered dam design, maintenance and repair, plus HDPE pipe welding, lining and rehabilitation.

03

Maintenance & Site Support

Mechanical, electrical and instrumentation maintenance assistance, including conveyor belt splicing, pumps, feeders, mills, flotation cells and process controls.

04

Cleaning & Environmental Support

Office, bank, plant, workshop and mine-site cleaning, rubble removal, housekeeping and services aligned with safety and environmental standards.

05

Haulage & Material Transport

Transportation of materials, equipment and goods according to client needs, supported by practical site-handling capability.

06

Crane Operator Services

Professional crane operation support for lifting, loading and site handling tasks, with disciplined attention to planning and safety.
